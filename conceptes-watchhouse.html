Aeropress.

A simple yet versatile immersion brewer with ample opportunity to explore. Here’s our recipe to get you started.

3m

Step 01. Prepare.

Using hot water, rinse the paper filter to remove any taste.

Place the plunger in the AeroPress and flip it so it’s upside down.

Carefully weigh out 15g of coffee. If using beans, grind to medium fine.

Step 02. Bloom.

Start your timer and pour 60g of filtered water into the AeroPress. Stir gently to saturate all the coffee.

Leave to bloom for 30 seconds, allowing the coffee to degas.
