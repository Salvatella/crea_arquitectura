Homes

Discover the project ↓

The refurbishment of a century-old house and a contemporary extension to create two distinctive homes.

Complex commission to refurbish a century-old terraced house and add a modern extension, creating two very different homes grouped within a single scheme.

The intervention recovers the charm of exposed vaults and a basement wine cellar in the existing building, contrasting them with a modern interior organised around a double-height space in the extended home.

Each house has a rear garden with a pool, and the two share communal parking for two vehicles.

Project and site management.
José Luis de la Fuente and Jordi Nadal, architects
