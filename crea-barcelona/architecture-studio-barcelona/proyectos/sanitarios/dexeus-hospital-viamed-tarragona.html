Gynaecology and an in-vitro fertilisation laboratory as an independent, functional and welcoming centre within a large private hospital.

Project integrated into a large private hospital for the gynaecology area and in-vitro fertilisation laboratory. It is conceived as an independent centre within the healthcare facility, with a functional design and natural style that create a warm, welcoming environment and place the patient at the centre of its operation.
