VALLDOREIX

Sant Cugat del Vallès · Vallès Occidental

Discover the project ↓

The comprehensive refurbishment of a suburban home that retains its domestic character and Noucentista spirit.

Comprehensive refurbishment project for a detached family home. The intervention clears the original building and extends its volume, retaining its domestic character and preserving some Noucentista features.

The proposal updates the volume and façade, introducing contemporary elements without entirely erasing the character of the original house. The interior is reorganised to create more open, functional and light-filled spaces, with a double-height space in the communal area.

Project and site management.
José Luis de la Fuente and Cristina Casabona, architects
Location.
Sant Cugat del Vallès, Vallès Occidental
