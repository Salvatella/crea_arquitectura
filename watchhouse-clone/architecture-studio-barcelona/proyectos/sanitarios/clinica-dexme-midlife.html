A women health and aesthetics centre conceived as a natural, warm and welcoming space.

Project for a new centre dedicated to women health and aesthetics during the stages of menopause.

An exclusive treatment and welcome space with private parking for the clinic. The aim is to create a warm, natural interior with materials and textures that move away from the clinical to become a comfortable, welcoming place with a personal approach for users.
