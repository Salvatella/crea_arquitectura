New outpatient clinics that modernise a landmark hospital building with natural light, warm colours and textures.

Interior refurbishment project integrated into the gradual functional and visual transformation of a landmark hospital building in Barcelona.

Starting from an existing building with a remarkably modern structure despite being more than 40 years old, the spaces are modernised and adapted to create new outpatient clinics. The intervention enhances natural light and the use of natural colours and textures to create warm, contemporary environments.
