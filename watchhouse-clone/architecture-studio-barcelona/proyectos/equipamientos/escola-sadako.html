A phased refurbishment of a leading school, conceived for new ways of teaching and learning.

Phased refurbishment project for a leading school. The proposal understands the educational model as a programme that requires open, transparent, multifunctional spaces adapted to new ways of teaching and learning.

The proposed spaces and solutions seek naturalness and simplicity as the best way to reach the real protagonists: the children.
