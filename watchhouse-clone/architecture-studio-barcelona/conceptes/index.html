La SELVA Home

La Selva del Camp, Baix Camp, 2003.

Intervention work on a century-old building: a farmhouse that had served as the main house and centre of a large agricultural estate.

The brief required preserving the image of a traditional house integrated into the landscape, while creating a modern and functional interior.

The proposal involved a complete interior clearance and the careful replacement of the traditional stone-wall structure with a modern steel structure, allowing generous spans and large rooms.

The result is a comfortable, welcoming home that respects and responds to its rural setting, ageing through colours and textures that will integrate into the landscape.

LA QUADRA Home

Bràfim, Alt Camp, 2015.

Refurbishment project for a house in a rural setting, based on an old building that had been used as an animal stable and hayloft.

The proposal starts with respect for the memory of the existing building. Its original structural elements are retained, consolidated and enhanced, preserving the character that reveals the passage of time and former uses.

An open, modern and informal living area is designed as a summer home. The intervention uses exposed materials such as concrete, timber, brick and steel, creating a fluid relationship between inside and out and maximising spatial continuity.

Refurbish

Architecture and construction techniques offer a valuable opportunity to give old and obsolete buildings new purpose, respecting the essence of the original construction and bringing new ideas for economic savings and environmental sustainability.

The interior is reorganised to create more open, functional and light-filled spaces. The project introduces a double-height space in the communal area and a horizontal window in the new upstairs bedroom.

The proposal updates the volume and façade of the home, introducing contemporary elements without entirely erasing the character of the original house.

VALLDOREIX Home

Sant Cugat del Vallès, Vallès Occidental, 2003.

Comprehensive refurbishment project for a detached family home.

Comprehensive refurbishment of a suburban home. The intervention clears the original building and extends its volume, retaining its domestic character and preserving some Noucentista features.

A clean, contemporary interior where the open kitchen, white surfaces and exposed brick wall live alongside the light projected from the balcony.

The proposal organises the home in a linear sequence of spaces, making use of natural light and encouraging cross-ventilation between the façade and the internal courtyard.

A renewal intervention on a large old apartment, divided into two symmetrical, compact and functional homes.
